🚀 快速开始

1. 进入演示目录:
   $ cd deploy/nexora-2.0-demo

2. 启动服务:
   $ ./start-demo-simple.sh

3. 测试查询:
   $ curl -X POST http://127.0.0.1:8080/api/query \
     -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
     -d '{"query": "MATCH (n) RETURN COUNT(n)"}'

4. 停止服务:
   $ ./stop-demo-simple.sh
